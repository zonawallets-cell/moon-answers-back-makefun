One line, 99 characters: the Moon's reply at last to everyone who has ever stared up at it. It answers the wishes, the howls and the late-night promises in one breath, then turns them around — four billion years of listening, and nobody ever wished the Moon goodnight back.

The entry:

> `You wished, you howled, you promised. Four billion years, and nobody ever wished me goodnight back.`

99 characters, one line, valid UTF-8 — inside the 240-character gate in `RULES.txt`.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/output python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`) and validates it against the gate (1–240 characters, single line, non-whitespace, valid UTF-8) before and after writing.
